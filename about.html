Who we are

The bridge between your e-waste and the right recycler

Businesses generate e-waste constantly — retired laptops, old servers, damaged UPS units, cable offcuts from projects. Most of it ends up in storerooms or with informal scrap dealers, creating data risks and compliance gaps.

E-WasteBiz Enterprises solves this. We evaluate your scrap, pay you a fair price, handle collection, and channel the material to government-authorised recyclers and refurbishers. You get value back, a clean storeroom and complete paperwork — without managing multiple vendors.

Based in Delhi-NCR, we serve corporate offices, IT parks, factories and institutions across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad.
