Privacy Policy

How E-WasteBiz Enterprises handles the information you share with us.

Information we collect

When you submit a pickup or quote request we collect the details you enter — such as your name, phone number, email, company, city and a description of your e-waste. We also record basic technical data (IP address, browser, the page and campaign you came from) to prevent spam and measure our advertising.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare quotes, arrange pickups, issue invoices and compliance documents, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & analytics

This website may use Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta pixel to understand visits and measure ad performance. These services may set cookies in your browser. Your theme preference is stored locally on your device.

Data on your devices

Data-bearing devices handed to us are handled under a chain-of-custody and wiped or destroyed before recycling or resale. Certificates of data destruction are available on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@ewastebiz.in.
